## Deliberately excluded from this phase

- GMC number until direct human confirmation against the GMC register is recorded.
- Qualifications until one canonical version is approved by the practice.
- A years-of-experience total.
- A current or lifetime robotic-procedure total.
- Claims such as best, top, leading, pioneering or record-breaking surgeon.
- Claims that robotic surgery is automatically better than laparoscopic or open surgery.
- Robotic availability for a specific patient, procedure or hospital without individual clinical and pathway confirmation.

## Maintenance rule

Recheck all source links and public wording whenever the corresponding page is materially updated. A newer institutional report can supplement this register, but it must not silently convert a dated programme report into a current personal claim.
